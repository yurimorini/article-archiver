# Article archiver

Eleanor keeps a copy of a page the operator chooses. The copy, the purified body, and the URL that names the page are different things.

## Language

**Archive**:
A stored copy of one page.
_Avoid_: Article, bookmark, snapshot

**Article**:
The purified body of a page.
_Avoid_: Archive, page copy

**URL pointer**:
A reference that names a page by its URL.
_Avoid_: Archive, article, bookmark
